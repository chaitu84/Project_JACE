Scope of the study

Computational evidence needs experimental follow-up.

Many overlapping CCU-rich windows can describe one repeat-rich region. Shuffling preserves nucleotide counts while allowing dinucleotide frequencies and potential structure to change. Biological binding and therapeutic activity remain experimentally untested.

Methods & reproducibility

The calculation behind the comparison.

Full RNA sequences, a shared control pool, and one best reported interaction per pair.

How were the candidates selected?

GENCODE v50 lncRNAs were screened for exact reverse-complement 10-mers against the 573-nt HPV16 E6*I/E7 spliced RNA region. The corrected search used 564 overlapping viral windows at step 1.

Shortlisting required at least three real matches, two nonoverlapping human sites, a real count above its shuffled mean, and an unadjusted empirical p-value ≤ 0.05. Selection used the same dataset being explored.

How were energies predicted?

IntaRNA 3.4.1 with ViennaRNA 2.7.0 compared each full human RNA with the original viral RNA and the same 100 saved viral shuffles. Inputs retained their original 5′→3′ order, with T converted to U.

Local accessibility windows were 150 nt with a maximum intramolecular base-pair span of 100 nt. Heuristic mode used a 7-base-pair seed. Each pair contributed one best reported interaction.

The energy combines hybridization with accessibility and opening costs. More negative values are more favorable within this model.

What does the energy tail fraction mean?

Count the shuffled energies equally or more favorable than the original, add one, and divide by 101.

(count(Eshuffle ≤ Eoriginal) + 1) / 101

With 100 controls, the minimum value and spacing are 1/101 ≈ 0.009901. This is an exploratory empirical energy comparison. It does not give the probability that binding is false.

What limits the interpretation?

The candidates were selected from the screening data. Four share extensive sequence, and overlapping repeat windows are correlated. The shuffle model preserves single-nucleotide composition without preserving natural RNA sequence organization.

None of the 197,195 screened transcripts passed Step 4 BH FDR 0.05. The energy controls leave that result unchanged. No experimental binding or therapeutic activity was established.
